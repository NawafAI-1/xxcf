WorldPop Saudi Arabia Gridded Population Density, 2000-2020

Annual 1 km resolution gridded population density rasters for Saudi Arabia (2000-2020), following WorldPop naming and grid conventions. Provides a spatially explicit, country-wide view of population distribution that can be clipped to Red Sea coastal governorates for human-pressure and coastal-development analyses.

Known issues (4)

  • •File naming (sau_pd_{year}_1km.tif) and directory context strongly match WorldPop's population density product, but the exact WorldPop sub-product (constrained vs. unconstrained, UN-adjusted vs. not) could not be confirmed from the files alone; original download provenance/README not present in the folder.
  • •Coverage is all of Saudi Arabia, not clipped to Red Sea coastal governorates; no Red-Sea-specific subsetting has been performed.
  • •No data for 2021-2024 (Rmd registry entry marks WorldPop as [Previous]/already acquired, but the local archive stops at 2020).
  • •Exact citation DOI not confirmed for this specific product/vintage, so left null rather than guessed.
