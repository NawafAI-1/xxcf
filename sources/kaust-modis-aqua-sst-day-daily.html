AQUA/MODIS Sea Surface Temperature (Daytime, 11 micron) Daily L3m: Red Sea

Daily NASA AQUA/MODIS Level-3 mapped (L3m) daytime sea surface temperature granules (11-micron longwave-infrared retrieval, standard SST product) covering the Red Sea, archived as a directory of daily NetCDF granules, a tar bundle of the same granules, and a single time-merged 'combined' NetCDF file.
