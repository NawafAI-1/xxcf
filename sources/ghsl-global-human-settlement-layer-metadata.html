Copernicus Global Human Settlement Layer (GHSL): Metadata Record

Metadata record describing the Copernicus Global Human Settlement Layer (GHSL), a global gridded dataset of built-up surface and population produced by the European Commission Joint Research Centre (JRC) from Sentinel-2/Landsat imagery and census data. Only the dataset metadata record has been downloaded locally; the underlying GHS-BUILT-S and GHS-POP raster grids themselves (2.0 GB and 4.9 GB global downloads respectively) have not yet been acquired.

Known issues (3)

  • •Only the dataset metadata CSV is present locally; the actual GHS-BUILT-S and GHS-POP GeoTIFF grids (2.0 GB and 4.9 GB) have not been downloaded yet.
  • •GHSL is a global product with no Red-Sea-coastal-specific subsetting or clipping performed locally.
  • •A separate GHS-BUILT-S citation/DOI also applies (Pesaresi & Politis 2023, doi:10.2905/9F06F36F-4B11-47EC-ABB0-4F8B7B1D72EA) but is not included here since this record's citation privileges the population product most relevant to the socio-economic domain.
