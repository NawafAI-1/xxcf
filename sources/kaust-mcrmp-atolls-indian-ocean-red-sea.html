Atolls of the Indian Ocean and Red Sea - Geomorphological Vector Data (MCRMP)

Shapefile-based geomorphological maps of coral reef atolls across the Indian Ocean and Red Sea region, from the Millennium Coral Reef Mapping Project (MCRMP), created by photo-interpretation of Landsat 7/8 imagery (Andrefouet, 2023). Distributed as per-region zipped shapefile bundles; only the Sudan bundle falls within the Red Sea bounding box, the remainder (BIOT/Chagos, Lakshadweep, Maldives, Seychelles) cover other Indian Ocean atoll systems but are archived together in this KAUST folder.

Known issues (4)

  • •n_rows values are the polygon/tile counts embedded in each zip filename (e.g. 'Sudan-2' = 2), not verified by opening the shapefiles.
  • •Dataset spatial extent is much larger than the Red Sea catalog bbox (includes Chagos/BIOT, Lakshadweep, Maldives, Seychelles); bbox widened to roughly span from the Red Sea to the western Indian Ocean rather than clipped to Red Sea only, since all files are bundled as one MCRMP release.
  • •Only the Sudan-2-ok.zip resource is directly relevant to the Red Sea subbasins; other regional resources included for archival completeness because they ship together in the KAUST folder.
  • •Temporal range (2001-2007) taken from the readme's funding period description, not from shapefile metadata.
