Data Saudi: National Economic Indicators (GDP, Prices, Labor, Markets)

National quarterly and monthly economic indicators for Saudi Arabia sourced from the Data Saudi open data portal, including nominal/real/seasonally-adjusted GDP by economic activity, wholesale price index, working hours by gender/nationality/sector, and Tadawul stock market indicators. Included for socio-economic context (e.g. agriculture/fishing sector GDP contribution) though most series are not marine- or coastal-specific.

Known issues (3)

  • •National-level macroeconomic indicators, not Red-Sea or coastal-specific; only the Agriculture/Forestry/Fishing sector series are directly relevant to marine socio-economics.
  • •Tadawul stock market indicators are only tangentially related to the Socio-Economic marine domain and are included here mainly for completeness since the file was present in the DataSaudi export folder.
  • •Downloaded as separate export files from the Data Saudi portal (filenames carry export timestamps of 2026-03-23); no formal citation string confirmed, so citation is left null.
