GASTAT Health Care Statistics 2025 (Saudi Arabia)

National tables from GASTAT's Health Care Statistics 2025 publication (46 tables), covering health expense coverage, health care visit rates, dental care, self-reported unmet health/dental needs, teleconsultation and digital health access, and long-term care for older adults, disaggregated by sex, nationality, age group, and administrative region. Based on the National Health Survey 2024 and the Woman and Child Health Survey 2024.
