GASTAT Health Determinants Statistics Publication 2024 (Saudi Arabia)

National tables from GASTAT's Health Determinants Statistics Publication 2024, covering obesity and overweight (BMI) among adults and children, tobacco/e-cigarette consumption and passive smoke exposure, and nutrition statistics including fruit and vegetable consumption frequency, disaggregated by sex, nationality, age group, and administrative region.

Known issues (3)

  • •Multi-sheet XLSX with 25 tables in wide, human-readable (not tidy) layout; would require reshaping before analysis.
  • •A near-duplicate file 'Health Determinants Statistics Publication 2024 EN (1).xlsx' (identical file size, 205,054 bytes) also exists in the same folder; only the canonical (non-'(1)') filename is cataloged as the resource; the two should be diffed/deduplicated before archiving.
  • •No sub-national spatial detail beyond GASTAT's administrative regions, which do not align with Red Sea subbasin boundaries; applied uniformly across all five subbasins.
