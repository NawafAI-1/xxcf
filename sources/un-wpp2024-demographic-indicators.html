UN World Population Prospects 2024: General Demographic Indicators (F01)

United Nations World Population Prospects 2024 revision, General file F01 'Demographic Indicators': annual population, fertility, mortality, and migration indicators for all countries and regions of the world, 1950-2023 (Estimates sheet), with additional projection-variant sheets to 2100. Includes Saudi Arabia as one of ~300 geographic entities; not Red-Sea or coastal-specific.

Known issues (4)

  • •Full global file (300 geographic entities, ~21,983 rows in the Estimates sheet alone); Saudi Arabia rows have not been extracted into a Red-Sea-specific subset.
  • •National-level indicators only; no subnational or coastal-governorate breakdown is possible from this source.
  • •Workbook also contains 13 additional projection-variant sheets (Medium/High/Low/Constant-fertility/etc., to 2100) not itemized in the variables list above; only the historical 'Estimates' sheet (1950-2023) is described here.
  • •Exact UN DESA DOI for WPP2024 not confirmed from the file itself, so left null rather than guessed.
