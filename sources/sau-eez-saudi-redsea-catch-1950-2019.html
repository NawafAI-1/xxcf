Sea Around Us -- Reconstructed Catch Data, Saudi Arabia (Red Sea) EEZ

Sea Around Us (SAU) reconstructed marine fisheries catch data for the Saudi Arabia (Red Sea) Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ ID 683), 1950-2019. Combines officially reported FAO landings with reconstructed estimates of unreported catch and discards, broken down by species, functional/commercial group, fishing entity, sector (artisanal/subsistence/industrial/recreational), gear type, and end use, with landed value estimates. A general-purpose spatial/statistical reference layer, not itself an ecological field survey, but commonly used alongside ecological survey data for fisheries-pressure context.

Known issues (4)

  • •Not a Red-Sea field survey -- this is a spatial/statistical reference/context layer (EEZ-wide reconstructed catch), included per project scope as a supporting dataset.
  • •SAU 'reconstructed' catch includes modeled/estimated components (see readme.txt), not purely observed data; uncertainty_score column present in source but not summarized here.
  • •domain set to ['ecological','production'] as a cross-cutting fisheries dataset; could arguably live solely under production -- flagged for review.
  • •A duplicate .zip archive of the unzipped folder also exists alongside it (SAU EEZ 683 v50-1.zip) -- not catalogued separately.
