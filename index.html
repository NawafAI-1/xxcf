ReefNet: Global Coral Reef Image Annotation Dataset (v1.0)

ReefNet is a large-scale, taxonomically enriched collection of annotated coral reef images compiled from many CoralNet sources worldwide, built for training and benchmarking machine-learning coral classification models. It includes a consolidated global label set, per-source metadata, and a dedicated Red Sea test partition (the 'RSG-TestDataset') contributed by Red Sea Global, collected in the Al-Wajh lagoon (~26.4N, 36.4E) in 2022 and used as a held-out evaluation set in the ReefNet paper. The dataset as a whole is global in scope; only the RSG-TestDataset partition is Red-Sea-specific.

nutrition-healthPublic

Global Dietary Database (GDD) 2018 Final Estimates — Country-Level Dietary & Nutrient Intake (incl. Seafood)

Modeled, survey-derived estimates of habitual dietary intake for 46+ foods, beverages, and nutrients, disaggregated by country, age, sex, urbanicity, and education level. The local archive contains the full country-level, global, and regional estimate releases (one CSV per dietary factor, e.g. v11 = Total seafoods, v30 = Seafood omega-3 (n-3) fat), plus the official codebook. For the Red Sea nutrition-health domain, the seafood intake (v11) and seafood omega-3 fat (v30) variables for Saudi Arabia (ISO3 SAU) are the primary variables of interest, but the archive includes all other GDD dietary factors as well.

nutrition-healthPublic

Global Estimation of Dietary Micronutrient Inadequacies — National & Subnational Prevalence Estimates

Modeled national and subnational prevalence estimates of inadequate micronutrient intake (15 micronutrients including calcium, iron, zinc, vitamin B12, iodine, etc.) for most countries worldwide, including Saudi Arabia, based on the modeling analysis published in The Lancet Global Health (Passarelli et al. 2024). Includes the full companion GitHub repository (code, data inputs, figures, tables, and Shiny app source) as archived locally.
