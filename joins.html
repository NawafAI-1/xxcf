What joins to what

A catalogue lists datasets. The useful question is which of them can be put together. This page answers it from two signals: the concepts the records declare their variables feed, and the columns those variables would be joined on, filtered down to pairs that actually overlap in time and space.

What the catalogue cannot tell you yet

A join needs a column to join on, and the catalogue only knows about columns someone wrote down. Of 43 records, this many document each kind:

  • Time8
  • Coordinates3
  • Named place10
  • Species5

So for most records the answer is not “these cannot be joined” but “nobody has written down enough to say”. Documenting the time and location columns of the remaining records is the cheapest thing that would make this page useful across the whole catalogue.
